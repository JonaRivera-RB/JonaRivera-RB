### About me

I'm a mobile developer with a background in Telematics Engineering and a strong focus on the Apple ecosystem. I enjoy turning real-world ideas into clean, intuitive applications—designing the interface, connecting APIs, organizing the architecture, and polishing the details that make an app feel right.

Most of my work is centered around **Swift and iOS**, with additional experience building Android applications in **Kotlin** and backend services in **JavaScript**. Over the years, I've explored everything from social and productivity apps to reusable networking tools and location-based experiences.

What matters to me: thoughtful architecture, readable code, constant learning, and creating software that is genuinely useful.
